## The challenge

A manufacturer with operations in several countries was held back by legacy systems and separate tools in each department. Production was delayed, inventory records didn't match reality, and managers couldn't get current data to plan with.

<!-- TODO: add client-approved detail (sites, previous systems). -->

## Our approach

We deployed a cloud ERP with modules for production scheduling, inventory tracking and forecasting, supply-chain collaboration and finance, all on shared real-time data across sites. Routine tasks are automated, and each team gets its own dashboards.

Supervisors manage work from mobile devices on the shop floor, and the platform runs with security controls and automated backups.
