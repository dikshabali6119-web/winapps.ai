## The challenge

A group of enthusiasts wanted a social platform for their hobby, away from the noise of mainstream networks where focused discussion got diluted. Members wanted deeper conversations, safe spaces with real moderation, and a way to organise events.

<!-- TODO: add client-approved detail (communities, launch). -->

## Our approach

We built a mobile-first social app around customisable groups, with content feeds ranked for relevance, an events calendar and secure private messaging. Moderators get tools to keep discussions on topic, and members control their own privacy.

Ad-free premium tiers give the community a revenue model, and the platform can host many niches side by side.
