## The challenge

An international home-furnishings chain grew quickly and its operations didn't keep up. Manual processes left store and online inventory out of step, so some products were overstocked while others sold out, and the business lacked data to spot trends.

<!-- TODO: add client-approved detail (store count, markets). -->

## Our approach

We implemented a cloud ERP with modules for inventory, point of sale, customer loyalty and analytics. Stock, sales and loyalty data sync in real time between physical stores and the online shop, and supply-chain tracking shows what's on the way.

Custom promotion tools and sales forecasting help the merchandising team plan, and the platform is built to add new countries as the chain expands.
