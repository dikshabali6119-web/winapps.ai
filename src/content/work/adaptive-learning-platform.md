## The challenge

An education provider specialising in online courses was struggling with low student engagement and high dropout rates. Its one-size-fits-all programmes didn't respond to how each student was progressing, and educators had little data to step in early.

<!-- TODO: add client-approved detail (student numbers, programmes). -->

## Our approach

We built a learning platform that adjusts content delivery and assessments to each student's performance and preferences. It includes interactive modules, virtual classrooms, group tools and gamification, and runs in the cloud so students can learn on any device.

Educators and administrators see real-time progress analytics, so they can target support where it's needed.
